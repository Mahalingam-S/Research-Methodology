PAPER 1: OVERHEAD CAMERA INFRASTRUCTURE & ZERO-COST DEPLOYMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
File Name: 1-s2.0-S1877050925026845-main.pdf
Title: Smart CCTV Exam Monitoring to Detect and Alert Suspicious Activities 
       Using Deep Transfer Learning
Publisher: Elsevier Procedia Computer Science (2025)

WHERE TO READ & WHAT TO LOOK FOR:
1. Abstract (Page 1):
   - Read the 4-sentence summary.
   - Note the core idea: Using pre-installed overhead CCTV cameras to monitor 
     exam halls and detect suspicious actions (looking around, phone use, talking).

2. Section 3 - Methodology / Framework Setup:
   - Look at the Camera Placement Diagram.
   - Note how wide-angle overhead cameras record student positions without needing 
     expensive micro-cameras on every desk.

3. Section 4 - Experimental Results & Accuracy:
   - Check their accuracy metrics (achieved high classification accuracy across 
     suspicious behavior categories using transfer learning models).

HOW TO SPEAK ABOUT PAPER 1 IN YOUR PRESENTATION:
"Paper 1 proves that existing laboratory CCTV and overhead HD cameras can be 
repurposed for real-time exam monitoring, supporting our zero-hardware-cost 
deployment model ($0 additional hardware expense)."

PAPER 2: REAL-TIME OBJECT DETECTION & CHEATING TAXONOMY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
File Name: s41598-025-18412-0.pdf
Title: Online Exam Cheating Detection and Blockchain Trusted Deposit Based on YOLOv12
Publisher: Nature Scientific Reports (2025)

WHERE TO READ & WHAT TO LOOK FOR:
1. Abstract (Page 1):
   - Focus on how YOLO object detection is used to identify prohibited items 
     (smartphones, devices) and cheating behaviors in real time.

2. Section on Object Detection Model & Taxonomy:
   - Check how YOLO bounding boxes identify forbidden objects on student desks.
   - Note how real-time frame inference catches cheating gadgets immediately.

3. Section on Results & Detection Efficiency:
   - Note their high detection mAP (mean Average Precision) and real-time FPS score.

HOW TO SPEAK ABOUT PAPER 2 IN YOUR PRESENTATION:
"Paper 2 validates our 4-class physical malpractice taxonomy, demonstrating that 
lightweight YOLO models effectively detect prohibited gadgets (phones, notes) 
in video streams in real time."

PAPER 3: SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & TAXONOMY OF ACADEMIC DISHONESTY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
File Name: s44217-026-01275-6.pdf
Title: A Comprehensive Review of the Changing Landscape of Academic Dishonesty 
       in Automated Proctoring in the Era of Artificial Intelligence
Publisher: Discover Education / Springer (2026)

WHERE TO READ & WHAT TO LOOK FOR:
1. Abstract (Page 1):
   - Understand how modern AI tools change cheating tactics and why static 
     webcam proctoring is no longer enough.

2. Section on Visual Proctoring Modalities & Frameworks:
   - Skim the breakdown of visual sensing: Head Pose Estimation, Eye Gaze, and 
     Biometric Identity Verification.

3. Section on Research Gaps & Challenges:
   - Read the two main limitations of current AI proctoring:
     a) High procurement / hardware costs per student desk.
     b) Black-box AI alerts (lack of explainability for flagged cheating).

HOW TO SPEAK ABOUT PAPER 3 IN YOUR PRESENTATION:
"Paper 3 provides the theoretical backing for our 7-stage sequential processing 
pipeline and highlights key research gaps—specifically high hardware costs and 
unexplainable AI alerts—which our Grad-CAM and SHAP framework directly resolves."
