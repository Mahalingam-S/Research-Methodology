1. THE 4 TECHNICAL COMPONENTS YOU MUST MASTER
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMPONENT 1: YOLO OBJECT DETECTION (v8 / v11 / v12)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- What it does: Detects physical prohibited items on student desks (smartphones, 
  smartwatches, cheat sheets, extra papers).
- How it works: YOLO is a single-stage object detector that splits the video 
  frame into a grid and simultaneously predicts bounding boxes (x, y, w, h) and 
  class confidence scores.
- Key Metrics to Memorize:
  * mAP@0.5 (Mean Average Precision): Measures detection accuracy across object 
    classes at 50% IoU.
  * IoU (Intersection over Union): Area of Overlap / Area of Union.
  * FPS (Frames Per Second): Real-time speed (>= 25 FPS).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMPONENT 2: OVERHEAD CCTV PERSPECTIVE & ROI DESK SEGMENTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- What it does: Uses 1 ceiling CCTV camera feed to monitor 20–30 student desks 
  simultaneously.
- How it works: Defines a Region of Interest (ROI) bounding box around each desk 
  (Desk_1, Desk_2, ..., Desk_N).
- Why it's crucial: Achieves $0 ADDITIONAL HARDWARE COST because institutions do 
  not need to purchase or wire individual webcams for every student computer.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMPONENT 3: HEAD POSE ESTIMATION (YAW, PITCH, ROLL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- What it does: Tracks candidate 3D head orientation in real time.
- Key Angles:
  * Yaw: Horizontal head rotation (left/right). Main angle for looking at neighbor.
  * Pitch: Vertical head rotation (up/down). Looking down at desk vs up.
  * Roll: Head tilt (left/right).
- Threshold: Yaw angle > 30° toward adjacent desk = Gaze Deviation.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMPONENT 4: MOUTH ASPECT RATIO (MAR) & LIP MOTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- What it does: Calculates mouth opening frequency to detect talking.
- Formula:
  MAR = (|P2 - P8| + |P3 - P7|) / (2 * |P1 - P5|)
  (where P1...P8 are 2D/3D facial landmark points around lips).
- Threshold: MAR > 0.35 sustained over 5 consecutive frames = Mouth Opening.


================================================================================
2. YOUR NOVELTY FEATURE: FALSE POSITIVE ELIMINATION
================================================================================

- The Problem: If a student silently reads a difficult question to themselves, 
  their lips move (MAR > 0.35). Naive computer vision models falsely mark them 
  as cheating.

- Your Solution (Multi-Condition Rule Engine):
  Cheating Alert = (MAR > 0.35) AND (Head Yaw Rotation > 30°) AND (Gaze Target != Own Desk)

- Why Panel Will Praise This: It proves you designed a smart system that 
  understands real exam conditions instead of just building a naive tutorial.
